Admission Process

We've kept our admission process simple so you can focus on what matters most — your child's future.

1

School Visit

Visit our campus, meet our teachers and see our classrooms first-hand.

2

Form Fill Up

Submit the completed admission form along with required documents.

3

Admission Confirmation

Complete admission formalities and fees to confirm your child's seat.

Documents Required

  • ✓ Duly filled admission form
  • ✓ Birth certificate (photocopy)
  • ✓ Previous school's report card / transfer certificate (if applicable)
  • ✓ 2 recent passport-size photographs
  • ✓ Parent/guardian NID Card photocopy
  • ✓ Parent/guardian 2 passport-size photographs
